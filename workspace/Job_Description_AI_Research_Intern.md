# Job Description: AI Research & Systems Intern
**Company:** AnthroMetrics AI Research Labs  
**Department:** Autonomous Agent Safety & Local Intelligence Group  
**Location:** San Francisco, CA / Hybrid  
**Term:** Fall 2026 / 6-Month Co-op  

---

## Role Overview
We are looking for a driven AI Systems & Research Intern to join our Autonomous Agent Safety team. In this role, you will pioneer techniques for on-device reasoning, verifiable agent tool calling, deterministic sandboxing, and local retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) using small language models (SLMs).

---

## Responsibilities
1. **Local Agent Architecture:** Develop and benchmark multi-step reasoning frameworks that operate completely offline using local models (e.g., Qwen, Llama 3.2).
2. **Deterministic Tool Gating:** Build permission check layers and post-execution verification hooks to guarantee that agents cannot take unauthorized destructive actions.
3. **Retrieval Engine:** Optimize dense/hybrid document indexing on local filesystems with sub-second latency and strict citation provenance.
4. **Auditability & Observability:** Implement comprehensive, tamper-evident audit logging for every step in the agent reasoning and action loop.
5. **Evaluation:** Benchmark plan validity, JSON compliance, and tool safety under edge cases.

---

## Required Qualifications
- Strong proficiency in Python, AsyncIO, and deep familiarity with PyTorch or Transformers.
- Practical experience with local model inference engines (e.g., Ollama, vLLM, llama.cpp).
- Hands-on experience building RAG pipelines (chunking strategies, embeddings, vector indexing, citation extraction).
- Solid understanding of agent architectures, structured outputs (JSON schema enforcement), and deterministic tool interfaces.
- Familiarity with human-in-the-loop workflows and safety-critical execution verification.

---

## Preferred Qualifications
- Experience building full-stack prototypes with FastAPI and interactive web dashboards.
- Published research or open-source contributions in agent safety, tool-use reliability, or local RAG.
- Deep commitment to user privacy and local-first computing paradigms.
